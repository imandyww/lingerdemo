# Three-minute presenter script

The `/demo` route is deterministic, uses local fixtures, does not need credentials, and works without microphone permission. Starting the demo resets its local fixture automatically.

## 0:00–0:25 — Consent and purpose

Open `/demo`. Say: “Linger catches family conversations that almost happened. It never listens passively.” Point out **No audio is recorded** and **Nothing is saved before review**. Choose **Start the conversation**; this also resets the local fixture so the path is repeatable.

## 0:25–1:15 — A meaningful memory

Advance the scripted exchange. Let each partial transcript settle before the final line:

1. Grandma: “The rain today reminds me of the day I left home.”
2. Linger: “Who was with you?”
3. Grandma: “My younger brother Ming came with me to the station, but he was not allowed to board the train.”
4. Linger: “How old were you then?”
5. Grandma: “Seventeen. I had one suitcase and a red scarf my mother made.”
6. Linger: “Did you ever see that station again?”
7. Grandma: “No. But I still remember Ming standing there in the rain.”

Point out that Linger asks one concrete question, uses details already shared, and tolerates pauses. The mock passes final utterances only; partial text never drives generation.

## 1:15–1:55 — Review before saving

Choose **Simulate memory extraction**. Show:

- “The day I left home.”
- Ming as younger brother and Mother as the red-scarf maker.
- Hometown train station.
- “1968 — Left home by train at age seventeen.”
- “What happened to Ming after that day?”

Read the date provenance: the transcript directly states age seventeen; Grandma’s stored birth year is 1951; the year 1968 is derived with visible confidence. It is not invented from the transcript. Make a small correction if time allows, then explicitly confirm consent and choose **Confirm and save story**. The new story defaults to private; sharing is a separate choice.

## 1:55–2:30 — One archive, three views

Show the new story card. Switch to Timeline and point to the linked 1968 event. Switch to Family Tree and select Ming, now connected as Grandma’s younger brother with the source memory and unresolved question. Mention that story, people links, event, and question save in one transaction.

## 2:30–3:00 — AI gets out of the way

Open Family Gathering and choose the new card:

“Ask Grandma what happened to her younger brother Ming after she left home.”

Choose **Start the family conversation**. Linger introduces that one question, then becomes quiet. Point to the explicit **Recording is off** indicator and the separate action to intentionally begin preserving a new memory.

Close with: “Memory becomes archive; archive begins a family conversation; that conversation can become a new memory.”
